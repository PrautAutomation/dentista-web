What a first visit looks like

  1. Welcome and conversation

    First, we'll talk about what's bothering you or what you'd like to address. No rush, no judgment.

  2. Examination

    We'll look at the state of your teeth, and take an X-ray if needed, so we know where we stand.

  3. Care plan

    We explain what we propose and why, including an indicative price, before we start anything.

  4. Treatment or a follow-up appointment

    Depending on what's needed. Either right away, or we schedule a follow-up visit.
