How payment works here

Covered by insurance
Public health insurance covers the procedure in full, you pay nothing extra.
Co-payment
Insurance covers a fixed amount, you pay the difference. Typically for better materials or more demanding procedures.
Full price
Insurance does not cover the procedure, you pay for it in full. Most commonly aesthetic procedures and implantology.
